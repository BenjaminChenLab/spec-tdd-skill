Payouts: the batch calls payout_action once per payout per night and acts on the
string. Weekend payouts defer to the next business day before any other
consideration — KYC is re-checked on the execution day, so a weekend payout for
an unverified account still defers today. Large means at or above the 100000-cent
escalation threshold. Everything else follows the module spec exactly: pure
function, the seven weekday codes, ValueError on any bad input, single-digit
string returns.
